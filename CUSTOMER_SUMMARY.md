# Kirana annual report assistant

**For Kirana Logistics Group’s investor relations team**

Analysts can ask a question about an annual report and receive a short answer with the page and the relevant excerpt. The assistant also flags questions for which it lacks evidence. This gives the team a quick route to the source before sharing a figure with investors.

## What the pilot covers

The pilot uses the 2025 Kirana annual report. It checks narrative text, financial and operating tables, and text that can be read from images. The source matters when two pages disagree. The CEO letter reports 96 electric trucks added in 2025, while the errata corrects the figure to 82. An analyst can check the correction directly on page 12. The report also gives standard storage rates and refers volume discounts to a separate schedule. An analyst needs that schedule to confirm a discount.

In a 20-question pilot, source review confirmed 13 of 16 questions with known answers, and the assistant declined all four questions whose answers were absent. It correctly found the cold-chain storage rate of THB 780 on the scanned tariff page. Two errors involved applying a figure to the wrong country or period. After a change to check these cases, the assistant declined both questions in follow-up tests. The results describe this report and should be checked on a second report before a wider trial.

## Where an analyst should review

Check the cited page before using an answer externally. Small print, scan quality and chart layout can affect which values the assistant reads. Check the year and unit when a question involves a financial number. A source excerpt can be genuine while leaving part of an answer unsupported. When the assistant cannot answer, an analyst can check the page directly or provide the missing document.

## Recommended next step

Try the assistant on the next Kirana report with a small set of real analyst questions. Include figures that change across years, references to missing schedules, and at least one corrected number. Have the investor relations team review the answers and the excerpts before using the tool in its external reporting workflow.
